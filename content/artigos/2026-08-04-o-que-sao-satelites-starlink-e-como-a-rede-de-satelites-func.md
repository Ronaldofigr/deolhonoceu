<!--lang:en-->

Imagine you could spread thousands of Wi-Fi routers across the sky, covering every corner of the planet. That is exactly what SpaceX, the company founded by Elon Musk, is building with the Starlink project. It consists of a constellation of small satellites — each roughly the size of an office desk — that orbit Earth at about 550 kilometers altitude, much lower than traditional telecommunications satellites. This proximity makes all the difference: information travels a much shorter distance, reaching the user with far less delay, almost like a real-time conversation rather than a long-distance echo.
